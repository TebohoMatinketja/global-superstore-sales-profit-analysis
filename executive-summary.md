# Executive Summary: Global Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## Business Overview
This project analyses Global Superstore sales and profit performance across product categories, regions, customer segments, markets, countries, and time periods. A dashboard presents key performance indicators and comparisons to support business decision-making.

## Key Findings
The dashboard reports approximately 12.64M in total sales, 1.47M in total profit, and 25K total orders. Technology is the leading sales category at approximately 4.7M. The Central region leads regional sales at approximately 2.8M. The Consumer segment contributes the most profit at approximately 0.75M. Profitability varies by sub-category, with Tables showing a negative profit contribution.

## Business Risks
The business may face losses from unprofitable products, concentration of performance in stronger regions or customer segments, and challenges planning around fluctuating sales.

## Business Opportunities
Potential opportunities include growing Technology sales, improving the profitability of loss-making products, and increasing sales in lower-performing regions and customer segments.

## Recommendations
Management should review pricing, costs, discounting, and shipping for loss-making products; investigate what drives strong performance in the Central region; target growth in other regions and customer segments; and use historical sales patterns to improve forecasting and inventory planning. The dashboard's Profit Margin KPI should also be validated because the displayed value appears to repeat Total Profit rather than show a percentage.

## Conclusion
The dashboard helps identify performance drivers, risks, and areas for improvement. Using these findings to guide product, regional, and customer-segment strategies can support better decisions and sustainable business growth.
